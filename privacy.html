Privacy notice

Early-access list · Last updated: November 2026

In short: we collect only what we need to keep you updated about NaijaPocket, we never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

NaijaPocket is an early-stage Nigerian product helping people save money, find opportunities and stay safe online. For this early-access list, NaijaPocket is the data controller for the information you share with us.

What we collect

When you join the early-access list, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Your state of residence
  • The NaijaPocket modules you are interested in
  • Your consent, and the date and time you signed up

The budget teaser on our website runs entirely on your device. The numbers you type into it are not sent to us.

Why we collect it, and our lawful basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, as provided for under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA). We use it only to:

  • Tell you when early access opens and send you NaijaPocket updates on WhatsApp
  • Invite you to our WhatsApp community
  • Understand which states and features people care about most, so we build the right things first

What we will never do

  • We will never sell or rent your personal data.
  • We will not share it with advertisers.
  • We will not message you about things unrelated to NaijaPocket.

Where your data is stored

Sign-ups are stored in a private spreadsheet (Google Sheets) that only the NaijaPocket team can access. Google may process this data on servers outside Nigeria; we rely on Google's standard security and data-protection commitments for this transfer. We protect access with strong passwords and two-step verification.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until NaijaPocket launches and for as long as you remain interested. If you ask us to remove you, or if we decide not to proceed with the product, we will delete your data.

Your rights

Under the NDPA you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Withdraw your consent and ask us to delete your data at any time
  • Object to or restrict how we use your data
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your data has been mishandled

To use any of these rights, simply reply "STOP" or "DELETE" to any NaijaPocket WhatsApp message, or contact us at the address below. We aim to respond within 7 days, and always within the timelines set by law.

Children

The early-access list is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data, we will update this page and, for significant changes, let you know on WhatsApp before the change takes effect.
