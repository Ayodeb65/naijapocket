FAQ

Questions? We get am.

Still unsure? Follow our WhatsApp Channel for updates, tips and launch news.

What exactly is NaijaPocket?

An everyday assistant for Nigerians that helps you save money, find genuine opportunities and protect yourself — all in one simple app that works in your phone's browser.

Is it really free to join?

Yes. Joining early access costs nothing and needs no card. When NaijaPocket launches, a free plan will always be available. Pocket Plus and Pocket Pro are optional upgrades.

Do I need to download anything from the Play Store?

No. NaijaPocket is a web app: it opens in your phone's browser and you can add it to your home screen like a normal app. It's designed to stay light on data and work well on everyday Android phones.

When will it launch?

PocketSaver comes first, followed by PocketJobs. Early-access members will be the first to know — and the first to try it — through WhatsApp.

Where do the food prices come from?

PocketSaver will combine verified, estimated and community-submitted prices. Every price will be clearly labelled with where it came from, the location and the date — we'll never present an unverified price as confirmed.

What will you do with my phone number?

We'll use it only to send you NaijaPocket updates on WhatsApp. We never sell your data, and you can ask us to delete it anytime. Read our privacy notice.

Will PocketHealth diagnose illnesses?

No. PocketHealth will help you find clinics, set reminders and read general health information. It won't give diagnoses or prescribe medication — always see a qualified health professional.
